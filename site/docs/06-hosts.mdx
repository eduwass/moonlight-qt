---
title: Hosts
description: What makes a Mac and a Linux PC behave well as the machine being watched.
---

Any machine running Sunshine can be watched. The two below are the ones this was built and measured on, and what each needed. The scripts named here live in [eduwass/dotfiles](https://github.com/eduwass/dotfiles) under `infra/kvm`.

## A Mac

Sunshine on macOS needs three things to feel like a local desktop.

- **A display that follows the window.** `vdisplay` (in `mac-host/`) makes a virtual display and sets it to whatever size the stream asks for, at 2x, so a resized window gets a sharp picture and not a scaled one.
- **A build that keeps up.** Sunshine's ScreenCaptureKit capture, with a small patch (`mac-host/sunshine-local.patch`): the encoder's "real time" flag off (14 ms a frame at 3400 × 1912 instead of 17.5), and capture paced to the stream's rate.
- **Awake displays.** A Mac's displays sleep after a few idle minutes, and Sunshine then refuses a stream: "Failed to initialize video capture/encoding. Is a display connected and turned on?" Wake them first. As the device's **Before connecting** command:

```sh
ssh my-mac 'caffeinate -u -t 30 >/dev/null 2>&1 & sleep 1'
```

For the **instant pointer**, the host runs `cursor-share` (in `mac-host/`), which sends the cursor's shape to your Mac and tells Sunshine to leave its own cursor out of the picture.

`mac-host/install.sh`, run on the Mac, builds the helpers and sets up the services. `mac-host/doctor.sh` checks all of this from another machine.

## A Linux PC (Hyprland)

- **A display when there is no monitor.** Booted headless, Hyprland has no output and Sunshine finds no encoder. `pc/sunshine-virtual-display` makes a virtual output when Sunshine starts.
- **A display that follows the window.** The same script, as the prep command of Sunshine's Desktop app, sets that output to the size the stream asks for.
- **A display that stays that size.** Every time Hyprland rereads its configuration (a theme change, a scaling change, an edit) the virtual output falls back to 1080p and the stream would be stretched from it. `pc/sunshine-virtual-display-watch.service` puts the stream's size back, and keeps a scale you chose on the PC.
- **Awake and unlocked.** A blanked or locked PC streams black or its lock screen. `mac/pc-wake.sh`, as the **Before connecting** command, turns the output on, unlocks the session, and prints the PC's address on the home network when your Mac can reach it there.

The **instant pointer** does not work on Hyprland: it hands out a cursor's picture only when an application draws its own, not for the usual themed cursors. The PC's own cursor stays in the picture, one round trip behind your hand.

With an NVIDIA card stuck on an older driver, check that your Sunshine build's encoder still matches it (a GTX 1080 on driver 580 needs Sunshine 2026.516).

`pc/install.sh`, run on the PC, puts the configuration, the script and the two services in place. `pc/doctor.sh` checks all of this from another machine, including that what is installed is what is in the repository.
