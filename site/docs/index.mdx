MoonlightNext shows another computer's desktop in a window on your Mac. It is a fork of [Moonlight](https://moonlight-stream.org) and talks to [Sunshine](https://app.lizardbyte.dev/Sunshine/) on the other machine, but it is built for working on that machine, not for playing games on it.

![The device window: the machines to connect to, and the one selected](./images/devices.png)

## What it does that Moonlight does not

- **The picture is always one stream pixel per screen pixel.** Resize the window and the stream restarts in place at the new size, in one and a half to three and a half seconds, behind a still of the last frame.
- **A window of its own.** No title bar over the picture. A small tab at the top centre drops a bar with the connection, the switches and the traffic lights.
- **A list of your machines.** Each with a picture of what is on its screen, whether it answers, and how it is streamed.
- **Several streams at once**, each in its own window.
- **Links.** `moonlightnext://connect/My%20PC` opens a machine from Raycast, a script or a bookmark.
- **A doctor** that looks at the way to a machine and says what the link can carry.
- **Warm streams.** Close a window and have it back in under half a second.
- **One clipboard.** Copy on one machine, paste on the other: text and pictures. See [the device window](/devices#a-shared-clipboard).
- **An instant pointer.** Your Mac draws the pointer, so it moves with your hand and not one round trip later.
- **Something to look at from the first moment.** Connect, and the machine's last screenshot is in the window's place at once, until the stream is.
- **Windows that remember.** Each machine's window opens where you left it, as large as you left it.

## How fast it is

Measured from a pointer movement on the Mac to the change on its screen, at 60 frames a second:

| Stream | Host | Link | Hand to eye |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2560 × 1440 | 13" MacBook Pro (M1) | Thunderbolt | 37–38 ms |
| 3400 × 1912 | 13" MacBook Pro (M1) | Thunderbolt | 43–47 ms |
| 2560 × 1440 | PC, GTX 1080 | Wi-Fi | 37 ms |
| 3400 × 1912 | PC, GTX 1080 | Wi-Fi | 41 ms |
| 5120 × 2160 | PC, GTX 1080 | Wi-Fi | 51.5 ms |

More in [Performance](/performance).
