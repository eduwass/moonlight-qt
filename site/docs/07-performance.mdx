title: Performance
description: What was measured, on what, and what the numbers mean for choosing a size and a frame rate.

All numbers are from one desk: a MacBook Pro (M1 Max) watching, on a 5120 × 2160 monitor at 72 Hz.

## Hand to eye

The time from a pointer movement on the watching Mac to the change showing on its screen, median of 80 samples, at 60 frames a second unless said.

### A 13" MacBook Pro (M1), over Thunderbolt

| Stream | Hand to eye |
| --- | --- |
| 1920 × 1080 | 33–39 ms |
| 2560 × 1440 | 37–38 ms |
| 3400 × 1912 | 43–47 ms |
| 3840 × 2160 | 50–52 ms |
| 5120 × 2160, at 40 | 57–63 ms |

Its encoder takes 14 ms a frame at 3400 × 1912 and keeps up with 60 frames a second up to 3840 × 2160. Above that it gets 40.

### A PC with a GTX 1080, over Wi-Fi

| Stream | Hand to eye | PC time per frame |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1920 × 1080 | 37.5 ms | 4.5 ms |
| 2560 × 1440 | 37 ms | 6.6 ms |
| 3400 × 1912 | 41 ms | 9.2 ms |
| 3840 × 2160 | 46 ms | 11.0 ms |
| 5120 × 2160 | 51.5 ms | 14.2 ms |
| 6400 × 2700, at 40 | 68 ms | 20.5 ms |

It holds 60 frames a second up to 5120 × 2160. At 6400 × 2700 a frame takes it 19 ms at best: asked for 60 it delivers 50 with frames backing up, so it gets 50 there.

Over Tailscale instead of the home network, the same stream is about 4 ms slower.

## What the hand waits for

From the action to the first picture of the stream, on the watching Mac's clock.

| Action | 13" over Thunderbolt | PC over Wi-Fi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open a device by link, app not running | 2.1 s | 3.7–4.3 s |
| Resize the window | 3.5–4.0 s | 2.7 s |
| Bring a warm stream's window back | 0.4 s | 0.4 s |

The app's own window is up 0.7 s after it is started.

Of a connect to the PC, 1.7 s is Sunshine trying its encoders, which it does at every launch. Of a resize on the Mac host, 1.3 s is its virtual display taking the new size and capture starting again. About 1.3 s of any connect is the app starting.

## Where the time goes

At 3400 × 1912 from the PC, of 41 ms: 9 ms is the PC capturing and encoding, 4–6 ms the network, 3.5 ms decoding. The rest, about 23 ms, is waiting: for the host's next capture, for the next frame slot, and for the display.

## What to choose

- **A window, not fullscreen**, is the fastest way to work: fewer pixels, less time.
- **True Pixels** on a scaled monitor: the same sharpness on the glass for two thirds of the pixels.
- **A cable** where you can. Wi-Fi adds 4–6 ms when it is good and far more when it is not; [the doctor](/settings#doctor) tells you which.

## Reliability

On the builds these numbers come from: 30 of 30 window resizes on each host, 25 of 25 cold starts, an hour at 3840 × 2160 with a constantly changing picture with flat memory at both ends, and the app's memory flat across 20 resizes (117 MB).
